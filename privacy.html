Swipe Blast Privacy Policy

Updated: October 11, 2026
Developer: Okan Mert (Angel of Peace Studio)
Contact: rhythmswipe@gmail.com
Android app: com.okanmert.rhythmswipe

This policy describes the current game and the optional advertising and usage measurement features. Reading or accepting this policy does not grant permission for optional Analytics. Those choices are handled separately in the game.

1. Audience and local game data

Swipe Blast is designed for users aged 13 and older. Progress, scores, rewards, settings, and privacy choices are stored on the device. The game has no account registration or cloud player profile.

A neutral birth-year screen selects a local audience category. The entered year is not saved or transmitted. The year-only check selects the adult category only when all possible birthdays in that year would be at least 18 years old. Other users, including those who decline to state their age, use the protected category. This is a protective category selection, not identity or age verification.

2. Background content and configuration updates

All audience categories can receive level data and game configuration through Firebase Remote Config and Firebase Installations. These services also check version requirements. Downloaded content contains data rather than executable game code. Existing levels remain available if an update cannot be downloaded.

These Firebase services process technical information such as a Firebase installation identifier, application and SDK versions, operating system, language, country code and time zone. We use this processing to operate the game, deliver content, and maintain compatible versions. Optional Analytics permission is not required for content updates.

3. Advertising

In this version, advertising requests and mediation initialization are disabled for the protected category. Adult-category users may receive banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob and mediation partners AppLovin, ironSource, Pangle and Unity Ads.

Advertising providers may collect and share IP addresses, approximate location derived from IP, advertising or other device identifiers, device and app information, ad impressions and clicks, reward confirmations, and diagnostic, crash or performance information. Providers use this information for advertising, ad measurement, fraud prevention, security, and service operation. Provider behavior also depends on consent, device settings and the ad source serving the request.

Google User Messaging Platform presents advertising privacy choices where required, including applicable European regulations. You can reopen available advertising privacy choices from the game's Privacy menu and manage the advertising identifier through Android settings. Non-personalized advertising can still involve technical data and identifiers.

4. Optional basic usage measurement

Firebase Analytics is disabled by default. It is enabled only for adult-category users who explicitly choose Allow on the separate usage measurement screen. Declining does not prevent gameplay or content updates.

Our gameplay events measure level starts, successful level completions and foreground time spent playing levels. They include the level number or name and duration in seconds. Firebase Analytics also processes standard automatic app events, app-instance identifiers and technical app/device metadata while enabled. These are measurements of consenting app instances, rather than an exact count of every individual player. The app does not attach the entered birth year, a name, email address or custom user ID to gameplay events.

Analytics advertising storage, advertising user-data consent and advertising personalization consent remain denied. Analytics collection of the Android advertising ID is disabled. Google Signals, granular location/device data collection and Analytics advertising personalization are disabled in our Analytics property. These settings do not eliminate all technical identifiers or network metadata, and are separate from the AdMob advertising choices.

You can withdraw usage measurement permission in the game's Privacy menu. The app stops Analytics collection and resets Analytics data held locally by the SDK. A local reset does not itself erase data already processed on remote servers.

5. Local notifications

Game reminders are scheduled locally on the device, using the phone's language where supported and a fallback language otherwise. They are not Firebase push messages. Android may ask for notification permission. You can disable reminders in the game or notification access in Android settings.

6. Providers and sharing

Third-party providers process data under their own policies. Advertising transfers may qualify as sharing, sale or targeted advertising under applicable laws; use the available privacy controls to exercise relevant choices. We also disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect the game against abuse.

  • Google / Google Play services / AdMob / Firebase and Firebase privacy information
  • AppLovin privacy policy — for more information about AppLovin's collection and use of your information, visit this policy.
  • ironSource privacy policy
  • Pangle privacy policy
  • Unity game player and app user privacy policy

7. Retention and deletion

Local progress and settings remain until you clear app storage or uninstall the game, subject to any backup and restore behavior of your device. Clearing storage removes your progress. Provider advertising, diagnostic and Analytics data follow the provider's retention policies and applicable legal requirements.

For access or deletion requests, contact rhythmswipe@gmail.com and identify Swipe Blast and the relevant service or concern. Do not send a password, government identity document or unnecessary sensitive information. We will explain what data we control, assist with applicable requests and, when needed, direct you to the provider's request process. We cannot locate a provider record using only your locally entered birth year, and resetting the app is not a promise of server-side deletion.

8. Younger users

The game is not designed for children under 13. Teenagers and users whose adult status is not established use the protected category with advertising and optional Analytics disabled. Essential content/configuration services described above remain available. Local age categories do not replace additional requirements that may apply to minors in a user's country. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us.

9. Security, international processing and rights

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No transmission or storage method is completely secure. Providers may process information outside your country. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, or a complaint to your data protection authority. Contact us to discuss a request.

10. Changes and contact

We will update this page when our processing changes and update the date above. Material changes may require an in-game notice or renewed choices. For privacy questions, contact Okan Mert at rhythmswipe@gmail.com.
