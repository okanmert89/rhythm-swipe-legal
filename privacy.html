Swipe Blast Privacy Policy

Updated: October 20, 2026
Developer: Okan Mert (Angel of Peace Studio)
Contact: rhythmswipe@gmail.com
Android app: com.okanmert.rhythmswipe

This policy describes the current game and the optional advertising and usage measurement features. Reading or accepting this policy does not grant permission for optional Analytics. Those choices are handled separately in the game.

1. Audience and local game data

Swipe Blast is designed for users aged 13 and older. Progress, scores, rewards, settings, and privacy choices are stored on the device. The game has no account registration or cloud player profile.

Google Play Age Signals may request age-range sharing to provide age-appropriate game access. Shared signals indicating an age below 13 prevent access; mandatory verification is handled through Google Play. If a signal is unavailable or not shared, game access remains available. The game does not retain the returned age range or install ID, and does not use Play age signals for advertising, marketing, profiling or Analytics.

Separately, the privacy screen lets users optionally declare that they are 18 or older. Only this independent, locally stored declaration selects the adult advertising and optional Analytics profile. Without the declaration, conservative protected advertising settings apply. This declaration is not identity verification.

2. Background content and configuration updates

All audience categories can receive level data and game configuration through Firebase Remote Config and Firebase Installations. These services also check version requirements. Downloaded content contains data rather than executable game code. Existing levels remain available if an update cannot be downloaded.

These Firebase services process technical information such as a Firebase installation identifier, application and SDK versions, operating system, language, country code and time zone. We use this processing to operate the game, deliver content, and maintain compatible versions. Optional Analytics permission is not required for content updates.

3. Advertising

Adult-profile users may receive banner, interstitial and rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob and mediation partners AppLovin, ironSource, Pangle and Unity Ads. Protected-profile users, including users who do not declare an adult profile, use separate Google-only ad units with general-audience content filtering, child age treatment and publisher first-party ID disabled. Third-party mediation initialization is disabled for that session; these units are not assigned to mediation groups or campaigns. Advertising is requested only when Google's UMP SDK permits requests. Ad availability is not guaranteed.

Advertising providers may collect and share IP addresses, approximate location derived from IP, advertising or other device identifiers, device and app information, ad impressions and clicks, reward confirmations, and diagnostic, crash or performance information. Providers use this information for advertising, ad measurement, fraud prevention, security, and service operation. Provider behavior also depends on consent, device settings and the ad source serving the request.

Google User Messaging Platform presents advertising privacy choices where required, including applicable European regulations. You can reopen available advertising privacy choices from the game's Privacy menu and manage the advertising identifier through Android settings. Non-personalized advertising can still involve technical data and identifiers.

4. Optional basic usage measurement

Firebase Analytics collection stays off until the initial privacy flow is complete. It is available only for the independently declared adult profile. On a new installation the optional Analytics checkbox is preselected; you can clear it before continuing. An existing opt-out is preserved. Without an adult declaration Analytics remains off. Reading or accepting the Terms alone does not enable Analytics; the separate optional choice determines collection. Declining does not prevent gameplay, content updates or separately enabled notifications.

Our gameplay events measure level starts, successful level completions and foreground time spent playing levels. They include the level number or name and duration in seconds. Firebase Analytics also processes standard automatic app events, app-instance identifiers and technical app/device metadata while enabled. These are measurements of consenting app instances, rather than an exact count of every individual player. The app does not attach Play age signals, age declarations, a name, email address or custom user ID to gameplay events.

Analytics advertising storage, advertising user-data consent and advertising personalization consent remain denied. Analytics collection of the Android advertising ID is disabled. Google Signals, granular location/device data collection and Analytics advertising personalization are disabled in our Analytics property. These settings do not eliminate all technical identifiers or network metadata, and are separate from the AdMob advertising choices.

You can withdraw usage measurement permission in the game's Privacy menu. The app stops Analytics collection and resets Analytics data held locally by the SDK. A local reset does not itself erase data already processed on remote servers.

5. Local and push notifications

Game reminders can be scheduled locally on the device. Starting with version 109, game news and update messages can also arrive through Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). Earlier versions use local reminders only. Supported phone languages determine reminder text and the language topic used for push messages; unsupported languages use a fallback.

FCM uses a Firebase installation identifier and a registration token to route messages to this app installation. The selected language topic and technical delivery/service information are also processed to provide notifications. This notification preference is separate from optional Analytics and advertising privacy choices. Optional Analytics permission is not required to receive notifications. We do not attach Play age signals, an age declaration, a name or an email address to push messages. FCM delivery-metrics export to BigQuery is disabled in the app.

Android may ask for notification permission. You can turn notifications off in the game's Privacy menu or disable notification access in Android settings. Turning them off in the game cancels scheduled reminders and requests topic unsubscription and deletion of the FCM registration token. It does not delete the Firebase installation used by essential content/configuration services, or promise deletion of all information already processed by providers. Delivery requires service availability and may be delayed or fail.

Third-party providers process data under their own policies. Advertising transfers may qualify as sharing, sale or targeted advertising under applicable laws; use the available privacy controls to exercise relevant choices. We also disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect the game against abuse.

  • AppLovin privacy policy — for more information about AppLovin's collection and use of your information, visit this policy.

7. Retention and deletion

Local progress and settings remain until you clear app storage or uninstall the game, subject to any backup and restore behavior of your device. Clearing storage removes your progress. Provider advertising, diagnostic and Analytics data follow the provider's retention policies and applicable legal requirements.

For access or deletion requests, contact rhythmswipe@gmail.com and identify Swipe Blast and the relevant service or concern. Do not send a password, government identity document or unnecessary sensitive information. We will explain what data we control, assist with applicable requests and, when needed, direct you to the provider's request process. We cannot locate a provider record using only your local age declaration, and resetting the app is not a promise of server-side deletion.

8. Younger users

The game is not designed for children under 13. Teenagers and users whose adult status is not established use the protected category with conservative Google-only advertising and optional Analytics disabled. Essential content/configuration services described above remain available. Local age categories do not replace additional requirements that may apply to minors in a user's country. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us.

9. Security, international processing and rights

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No transmission or storage method is completely secure. Providers may process information outside your country. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, or a complaint to your data protection authority. Contact us to discuss a request.

10. Changes and contact

We will update this page when our processing changes and update the date above. Material changes may require an in-game notice or renewed choices. For privacy questions, contact Okan Mert at rhythmswipe@gmail.com.
